# B2B Partner Research

## Goal

Find potential B2B partners for the company.

## Process

1. Search for companies in the requested city.
2. Open their websites.
3. Determine what products and services they offer.
4. Check whether they serve businesses.
5. Look for contact information.
6. Evaluate whether they could be a potential partner.
7. Return a ranked list.

## Important

- Prefer information from the company's own website.
- If information is unavailable ignore company.
